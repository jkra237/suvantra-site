<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What the app processes on your device</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Rewrite PDF opens the PDF files you choose and edits them in your device's memory. Changes are always saved as a new file in a location you choose; your original stays unchanged. Text recognition for scanned pages also runs entirely on your device.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In addition, the app remembers a few settings on your device: the language you chose, whether the welcome message has been shown, how often you have tried a Pro feature and — if you want — your saved signature. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">When the app goes online</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Exactly one case: Rewrite PDF asks the Microsoft Store whether you have purchased Rewrite PDF Pro and, at your request, starts the purchase. This check runs through Windows and the Microsoft Store; we receive no data about you.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Beyond that, the app makes no connections. There are no update checks against our servers, no crash reports and no usage analytics. The links in the "About Rewrite PDF" window only open in your browser when you click them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
