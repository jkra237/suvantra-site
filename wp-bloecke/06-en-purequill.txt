<section class="wrap">
  <p class="augbraue">Autosave</p>
  <h2>Saved while you write.</h2>
  <p class="lead">You do not have to think about saving. Everything you write is saved as you
     go — on your computer, not in a cloud, and without being online.</p>
  <div class="saeulen" style="margin-top:30px">
    <div class="saeule">
      <h3>Continuously</h3>
      <p>About a second after your last keystroke, the text is saved on your computer.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="saeule">
      <h3>Checkpoints</h3>
      <p>Every 30 seconds the program records a checkpoint. The last twelve can be restored from the version history.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="saeule">
      <h3>Backup file</h3>
      <p>If you like, it also writes the whole state to a file at regular intervals — for example into a folder that Dropbox or OneDrive keeps in sync.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="flaeche">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="augbraue">Page layout</p>
    <h2>The page as it will be printed.</h2>
    <p class="lead">Not an endless flow of text but real pages in millimetres — with headers
       and footers, page breaks and margins you drag on the ruler. What stands on the
       screen stands on the paper.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="wrap">
  <p class="augbraue">Writing tools</p>
  <h2>Tools for writers.</h2>
  <p class="lead">A word goal shows how far you have come. A panel beside the text shows which
     words are piling up, and it weighs them: a word forty times across a novel is
     harmless, four times in one paragraph is not. Plus word count, sticky notes and
     a glossary for names and terms.</p>

     Tested on Ubuntu.</p>
