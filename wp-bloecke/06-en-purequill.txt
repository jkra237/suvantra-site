<section class="wrap">
  <p class="augbraue">Page layout</p>
  <h2>The page as it will be printed.</h2>
  <p class="lead">Not an endless flow of text but real pages in millimetres — with headers
     and footers, page breaks and margins you drag on the ruler. What stands on the
     screen stands on the paper.</p>
</section>

<section class="flaeche">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="augbraue">Repeated words</p>
    <h2>See what your editor will see.</h2>
    <p class="lead">A panel beside the text shows which words are piling up, and it weighs
       them: a word forty times across a novel is harmless, four times in one paragraph
       is not. Inflected forms are grouped together, function words are left out.</p>
    <figure class="schaubild">
      <picture>
      <source srcset="/assets/pq-en-wortwiederholungen.webp" type="image/webp">
      <img src="/assets/pq-en-wortwiederholungen.png" alt="The repeated-words panel beside the text" width="1366" height="768" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
    </picture>
      <figcaption>Every row names the forms found and the closest gap between two occurrences. A click jumps to the spot.</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="wrap">
  <p class="augbraue">Spelling</p>
  <h2>Seven languages, all inside the program.</h2>
  <p class="lead">Each with its own dictionary — nothing is fetched, nothing is sent to a
     service. You walk through the findings one by one, with suggestions and a personal
     dictionary for whatever the program does not know yet.</p>
  <figure class="schaubild">
    <picture>
      <source srcset="/assets/pq-en-rechtschreibung.webp" type="image/webp">

    <figcaption>English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Portuguese — interface and help as well.</figcaption>
  </figure>
</section>

<section class="dunkelband">
  <div class="wrap">
    <p class="augbraue">Scope</p>
    <h2>What is in it.</h2>
    <ul class="merkmale" style="margin-top:34px">
      <li>Fully offline — no account, no server, no subscription</li>
      <li>Autosave with checkpoints and version history</li>
      <li>Word goal, word count and repeated words in one panel</li>
      <li>Spell check with its own dictionaries in seven languages</li>
      <li>Sticky notes and a glossary beside the text, each in its own view</li>
      <li>Text clean-up: double spaces, empty paragraphs, line endings</li>
      <li>Seventeen colour schemes, focus mode, reading view, print preview</li>
      <li>Footnotes, tables, pictures, table of contents, handwritten signature</li>
      <li>Open: DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, Markdown, text</li>
      <li>Save as: DOCX, EPUB, PDF, HTML, Markdown, text</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <h2>System requirements</h2>
  <p>Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) and Windows 11, 64-bit. Around 400 MB on disk,
     most of it dictionaries. No internet connection needed — neither to set it up nor
     to work.</p>
  <p>Linux: a current 64-bit distribution, x86-64 or ARM64, around 300 MB on disk.
     Tested on Ubuntu.</p>
  <p class="klein">
    The browser version runs in Chrome, Edge and Firefox without installing anything.
    It is meant for trying the program out: headers and footers, margins and the print
    path are tools for a keyboard and a large screen, not for a phone.
