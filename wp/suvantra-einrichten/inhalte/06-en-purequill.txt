<section class="wrap eng">
  <h2>System requirements</h2>
  <p>Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) and Windows 11, 64-bit. Around 400 MB on disk,
     most of it dictionaries. No internet connection needed — neither to set it up nor
     to work.</p>
  <p class="klein">
    The browser version runs in Chrome, Edge and Firefox without installing anything.
    It is meant for trying the program out: headers and footers, margins and the print
    path are tools for a keyboard and a large screen, not for a phone.
