<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Version 1.3.0</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"klein"} -->
<p class="klein">17 October 2026</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">New</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>OpenDocument: files from LibreOffice and OpenOffice open completely, with fonts, lists, tables, pictures, footnotes, headers and footers — and documents can be saved as OpenDocument Text (.odt).</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Font and size apply to the selected text, as in Word. Word files that mix fonts and sizes open exactly as they were, word for word.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>New font picker: all fonts installed on your computer, recently used ones at the top, and a search as you type.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pictures have a new toolbar: in line with text, wrap text, behind text or in front of text. Pictures behind or in front of text move with their paragraph, so a signature stays under its closing line.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Lists work as in Word: the same indent, numbering 1. → a. → i., the style you chose last is used for new lists, and typing “a)”, “A.” or “I.” starts a list.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>PDF files carry a title, your name as author (“Your name” in the settings), a subject and keywords.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>On a phone, the browser version shows the text across the screen width, with one toolbar row and the menus behind ☰.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>New documents start in Calibri 12, and new users start with the “Cloud” colour scheme.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Fixed</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word files lost alignment, superscript and subscript, highlighting, link targets, page breaks, merged cells, captions and quotes.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pictures behind or in front of text came back from Word in line with the text, and a signature could land on the wrong page in Word.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Headings could be left alone at the bottom of a page; now they stay with their text. Paragraphs and lists break line by line at the end of a page, with widow and orphan control as in Word.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Capitalization after abbreviations works as in Word and can be undone.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>“Clear formatting” also turns headings and quotes back into normal text.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Lists pasted from Word keep their structure, and sub-items are no longer lost when saving.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Version 1.2.0</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"klein"} -->
<p class="klein">12 October 2026</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">New</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word files: logos and pictures in headers and footers, endnotes, different first and even pages, and formatting and tabs in headers are taken over. Word comments become notes beside the text.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Everything PureQuill does not show itself, such as columns, sections, text boxes and charts, is kept unchanged when you save the Word file again.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Headers and footers can hold your own logo in the size you choose, and the first page can have its own header and footer.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>New, compact “New document” window: title, page format and font at a glance, everything else under “More settings”. The page preview shows the formats to scale.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Settings with a sidebar: General, New documents, Autocorrect, Saving and Snapshots, each on one page. Saving shows at a glance what is active.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>“Reset to factory settings” resets the settings and keeps your documents, notes and glossaries.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The version history now reaches back up to a month. Recent snapshots stay complete, older ones are thinned out.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Keyboard shortcuts follow Word in your language, for example Ctrl+Shift+F for bold in German.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pasting text with pictures asks whether the pictures should come along.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Linux version as AppImage and .deb, for x64 and arm64.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Fixed</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Filled-in Word forms lost all answers when opened, and tracked changes went missing.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word files kept too little of their formatting: font colours, all caps, font sizes, indents, borderless tables and numbered lists that continue.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Printed pages all showed page number “0”.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Footnotes are printed at the foot of their own page instead of as a list at the end.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Long tables are split across pages on screen, as in print.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>A selected picture or a fully selected table can be deleted with Backspace or Delete.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Dialogs open centred in the program window.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Submenus closed only after a click; now they close when the pointer leaves them.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Pasted web pages and emails no longer push the layout apart.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The print preview showed the page in the colours of the scheme, or blank pages.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The character count counted paragraph breaks.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Double-clicking a .pqw file only opened the program, not the file.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Version 1.1.0</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"className":"klein"} -->
<p class="klein">4 October 2026</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">New</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>File › Save (Ctrl+S) writes the document to its Word file. The first time, it asks where to save; after that, it saves without asking. A .docx file you opened is updated directly.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Ctrl+S now saves the file. A checkpoint on its own is on Alt+Shift+S.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The status bar shows the file name with a dot: green when the file is up to date, in the accent colour when there are changes that are not in the file yet. A click saves.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Closing a document with unsaved changes asks first: Save, Don’t save or Cancel.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>If the file is locked, usually because it is still open in Word, the program says so and asks for another location.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>New documents start like in Word, with Calibri 11 and 1.15 line spacing. Font, size and spacing can be chosen right when you create a document.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>New section Settings › General › New documents, with “Reset to Word defaults”. Existing documents keep their look.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Line spacing is calculated the way Word does it, so 1.15 looks the same in both programs.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word, ODT and RTF files open in their own font, size, line and paragraph spacing, and keep them when saved.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The toolbar shows Bold, Italic and Underline with the letters Word uses in your language.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>New users start at 100% zoom, as in Word.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->

<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>File names no longer end with the punctuation of the first line, as in “Dear Sir or Madam,.docx”.</li>

<li>Spell check accepts typographic apostrophes, as in “don’t”.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word export: code blocks keep their line breaks and tabs.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Word export: lines no longer start with spaces that the editor did not show.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>The welcome window now closes after File › Open.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Creating or opening a document with no tab open no longer adds a second, empty tab.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Repeated words no longer lists filler words that are switched off.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->
<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>“Dictionary not available” is now translated into every language.</li>
