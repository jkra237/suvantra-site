What’s new in PureQuill Writer

Every released version, newest first.

Version 1.5.0

28 October 2026

New

  • Tables as in Word: merge and split cells, shading, borders, cell alignment and column width, right from the right-click menu.
  • Table styles in one click: Grid, Green, Blue, Orange, Grey and Simple, with highlighted header row, first column and banded rows.
  • Long tables repeat their header row on every page, as in Word.
  • Undo and redo work for every change, including tables, images, lists and paragraph formatting.
  • Word files split into pages as they do in Word: same line heights, tables in the same place, in print too.
  • Headers and footers are easier to edit: their own toolbar, page number and date visible while you type, logos, formatting and tab stops from Word, .odt and .rtf.
  • New “Insert” menu as in Word: image, table, link, page break, footnote and endnote, tables of contents, header and footer.
  • Notes on text passages redesigned; attached passages are only coloured while you point at the note.
  • Clearer settings, with “Reset all settings” under General.
  • Clear formatting without a selection works as in Word, and the image and table toolbars sit fixed below the main toolbar.

Fixed

  • Pinning a note to a passage inside a table could break the table.
  • Header and footer lines with a tab no longer gain an empty line, and clicking into the footer no longer moves a page number that is set with a tab.
  • Printing breaks tables at the same places as the screen and Word.
  • Old Word files (.doc) without spacing information get no space after paragraphs, as in Word.
  • On note cards in French and Spanish, the buttons for detaching and deleting had the same label.

Version 1.4.0

26 October 2026

New

  • Old Word files (.doc, Word 97–2003) open with pictures, tables, lists, footnotes, endnotes, headers, footers and comments.
  • RTF files open completely and can be saved as RTF. Word and OpenDocument templates (.dotx, .dotm, .dot, .ott), Word files with macros (.docm) and flat OpenDocument (.fodt) open too.
  • An opened .odt or .rtf file is saved back in its own format. The title bar shows the file name with its type, and formats PureQuill can only read show “[Compatibility mode]”, as in Word.
  • Word files look as they do in Word: small icons stay in the line, custom tab stops line up columns, lines under paragraphs and headings, character spacing, and list indents and bullet colours from the file.
  • Line and paragraph spacing per paragraph, as in Word: a new toolbar button with 1.0 to 3.0 and space before or after, and Format › Paragraph… (also in the right-click menu) with indents, special indent, spacing before and after, and line spacing single, 1.5 lines, double, at least, exactly or multiple.
  • Styles as in Word: Title, Subtitle, Heading 4 and three quotation styles, with a paragraph style list that shows each style as it looks.
  • Prepare e-book: finds chapter openings, tidies up text files (blank lines, line breaks in mid-sentence, quotation marks, dashes) and sets book typography for EPUB — in a copy, so the original stays as it is. The EPUB dialog shows whether a document is ready.
  • Find and replace redesigned, with Find and Replace tabs and match markers beside the scroll bar.
  • Typing ---, ___, === or *** and Enter draws a horizontal line, as in Word.
  • Duplicate a tab from its right-click menu, zoom with Ctrl + mouse wheel, and choose the snapshot interval under File › Snapshots › Automatic.
  • On Windows, PureQuill Writer appears under “Open with” for Word, OpenDocument, RTF, Markdown and text files.

Fixed

  • Pressing Enter on an empty list item leaves the list again; in coloured lists you could stay in the list forever.
  • Backspace at the start of the paragraph after a page break removes the page break, as in Word, instead of pulling the paragraph apart. A page break can also be selected with a click and deleted.
  • Headings with their own size were saved in the size of the heading style in Word, OpenDocument and RTF.
  • Lists from LibreOffice that fell apart in Word files are numbered correctly.
  • The dialog when closing a document asks as in Word — Save, Don’t save, Cancel — and Save is the default.
  • “Saved” shows its time after a restart too.

Version 1.3.0

17 October 2026

New

  • OpenDocument: files from LibreOffice and OpenOffice open completely, with fonts, lists, tables, pictures, footnotes, headers and footers — and documents can be saved as OpenDocument Text (.odt).
  • Font and size apply to the selected text, as in Word. Word files that mix fonts and sizes open exactly as they were, word for word.
  • New font picker: all fonts installed on your computer, recently used ones at the top, and a search as you type.
  • Pictures have a new toolbar: in line with text, wrap text, behind text or in front of text. Pictures behind or in front of text move with their paragraph, so a signature stays under its closing line.
  • Lists work as in Word: the same indent, numbering 1. → a. → i., the style you chose last is used for new lists, and typing “a)”, “A.” or “I.” starts a list.
  • PDF files carry a title, your name as author (“Your name” in the settings), a subject and keywords.
  • On a phone, the browser version shows the text across the screen width, with one toolbar row and the menus behind ☰.
  • New documents start in Calibri 12, and new users start with the “Cloud” colour scheme.

Fixed

  • Word files lost alignment, superscript and subscript, highlighting, link targets, page breaks, merged cells, captions and quotes.
  • Pictures behind or in front of text came back from Word in line with the text, and a signature could land on the wrong page in Word.
  • Headings could be left alone at the bottom of a page; now they stay with their text. Paragraphs and lists break line by line at the end of a page, with widow and orphan control as in Word.
  • Capitalization after abbreviations works as in Word and can be undone.
  • “Clear formatting” also turns headings and quotes back into normal text.
  • Lists pasted from Word keep their structure, and sub-items are no longer lost when saving.

Version 1.2.0

12 October 2026

New

  • Word files: logos and pictures in headers and footers, endnotes, different first and even pages, and formatting and tabs in headers are taken over. Word comments become notes beside the text.
  • Everything PureQuill does not show itself, such as columns, sections, text boxes and charts, is kept unchanged when you save the Word file again.
  • Headers and footers can hold your own logo in the size you choose, and the first page can have its own header and footer.
  • New, compact “New document” window: title, page format and font at a glance, everything else under “More settings”. The page preview shows the formats to scale.
  • Settings with a sidebar: General, New documents, Autocorrect, Saving and Snapshots, each on one page. Saving shows at a glance what is active.
  • “Reset to factory settings” resets the settings and keeps your documents, notes and glossaries.
  • The version history now reaches back up to a month. Recent snapshots stay complete, older ones are thinned out.
  • Keyboard shortcuts follow Word in your language, for example Ctrl+Shift+F for bold in German.
  • Pasting text with pictures asks whether the pictures should come along.
  • Linux version as AppImage and .deb, for x64 and arm64.

Fixed

  • Filled-in Word forms lost all answers when opened, and tracked changes went missing.
  • Word files kept too little of their formatting: font colours, all caps, font sizes, indents, borderless tables and numbered lists that continue.
  • Printed pages all showed page number “0”.
  • Footnotes are printed at the foot of their own page instead of as a list at the end.
  • Long tables are split across pages on screen, as in print.
  • A selected picture or a fully selected table can be deleted with Backspace or Delete.
  • Dialogs open centred in the program window.
  • Submenus closed only after a click; now they close when the pointer leaves them.
  • Pasted web pages and emails no longer push the layout apart.
  • The print preview showed the page in the colours of the scheme, or blank pages.
  • The character count counted paragraph breaks.
  • Double-clicking a .pqw file only opened the program, not the file.

Version 1.1.0

4 October 2026

New

  • File › Save (Ctrl+S) writes the document to its Word file. The first time, it asks where to save; after that, it saves without asking. A .docx file you opened is updated directly.
  • Ctrl+S now saves the file. A checkpoint on its own is on Alt+Shift+S.
  • The status bar shows the file name with a dot: green when the file is up to date, in the accent colour when there are changes that are not in the file yet. A click saves.
  • Closing a document with unsaved changes asks first: Save, Don’t save or Cancel.
  • If the file is locked, usually because it is still open in Word, the program says so and asks for another location.
  • New documents start like in Word, with Calibri 11 and 1.15 line spacing. Font, size and spacing can be chosen right when you create a document.
  • New section Settings › General › New documents, with “Reset to Word defaults”. Existing documents keep their look.
  • Line spacing is calculated the way Word does it, so 1.15 looks the same in both programs.
  • Word, ODT and RTF files open in their own font, size, line and paragraph spacing, and keep them when saved.
  • The toolbar shows Bold, Italic and Underline with the letters Word uses in your language.
  • New users start at 100% zoom, as in Word.

Fixed

  • Text from Word files looked larger than in Word.
  • File names no longer end with the punctuation of the first line, as in “Dear Sir or Madam,.docx”.
  • Spell check accepts typographic apostrophes, as in “don’t”.
  • Word export: code blocks keep their line breaks and tabs.
  • Word export: lines no longer start with spaces that the editor did not show.
  • The welcome window now closes after File › Open.
  • Creating or opening a document with no tab open no longer adds a second, empty tab.
  • Repeated words no longer lists filler words that are switched off.
  • “Dictionary not available” is now translated into every language.
  • The colour scheme dialog named the wrong scheme as the default.

Version 1.0.0

2 October 2026

First release on the Microsoft Store. Free, with no account and no subscription.

  • Opens DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, Markdown and text. Saves as DOCX, EPUB, PDF, HTML, Markdown and text.
  • Real pages: margins on the ruler, headers and footers, page breaks, A4 and US Letter.
  • Footnotes, tables, pictures, table of contents, handwritten signature.
  • Spell check with its own dictionaries in seven languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Portuguese.
  • Word goal, word count and repeated words in one panel.
  • Autosave with checkpoints and version history.
  • Sticky notes and a glossary beside the text.
  • Text clean-up, seventeen colour schemes, focus mode, reading view and print preview.
  • Interface and help in the same seven languages.
  • Settings › Save › Delete all data, which offers a full backup first.
  • An About window with the version and the licences of the dictionaries.
